International Pi Week 2022

MARCH 14 – MARCH 19, 2022

Continuing our annual tradition of commemorating the most well-known mathematical constant π, CMIT is back with loads of events to celebrate International Pi Day 2022. Instead of restricting ourselves to a single day, this year we had events happening for an entire week starting from March 14th.

CMIT Pi Day Talk Series

A four-part talk series featuring faculty and student researchers from 15th to 18th March 2022.

An Introduction to Conservation Laws

Speaker: Dr. Sudarshan Kumar K. (Faculty, School of Mathematics, IISER TVM)

Date & Time: March 15, 2022 | 3:00 PM IST

Venue: PSB Seminar Hall

Abstract: In this talk, we give an introduction to conservation laws, which are essentially scalar or systems of first-order partial differential equations. Several models arising in engineering applications and applied sciences can be described by equations of this form—for instance, traffic flow with abruptly changing road surface conditions, enhanced oil recovery process, weather prediction, and climate modelling. We begin with governing equations and mathematical aspects, followed by research problems arising in real-life applications.

Financialization of Savings

Speaker: Joyentanuj Das (PhD Student, School of Mathematics, IISER TVM)

Date & Time: March 16, 2022 | 3:00 PM IST

Venue: Online (Google Meet)

Abstract: This talk is tailored for anyone under 30 looking to understand financial planning, long-term compounding, and building a sustainable investment strategy.

Several Complex Variables vs. Single Complex Variables: A Jugalbandi

Speaker: Anand Chavan (5th Year BS-MS Student, School of Mathematics, IISER TVM)

Date & Time: March 17, 2022 | 3:00 PM IST

Venue: Online (Google Meet)

Abstract: We explore basic results from single complex variables and their extensions to several complex variables, highlighting where the two theories diverge as a motivation for studying function theory in higher dimensions.

Introducing Some Famous Problems of Number Theory

Speaker: Dr. Prem Prakash Pandey (Faculty, Department of Mathematical Sciences, IISER Berhampur)

Date & Time: March 18, 2022 | 3:00 PM IST

Venue: Online (Google Meet)

Abstract: Mathematics is a kingdom without a king, but it has a long-reigning queen: Number Theory. This talk introduces classic, famous problems in number theory alongside a glimpse into recent developments in the field.
