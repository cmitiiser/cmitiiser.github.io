GAME NIGHT!

WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 30, 2026

The Club of Mathematics (CMIT) hosted Game Night, bringing students together for an evening of puzzles, betting strategies, and applied math challenges.

Teams competed across three rounds designed to test speed, intuition, and deduction under time limits.

Games & Challenges

1. The Origami Marathon

Participants folded as many recognizable origami figures as they could before time ran out. Judges scored entries on both speed and fold quality.

Rules & Criteria:

  1. Final scores balanced total models folded against penalties for sloppy creases.
  2. Folds had to be crisp, symmetrical, and accurate.

Winner: Aryan (B26)

2. Find the Bluff

In each round, teams reviewed four math statements: three true and one fake. Teams had to spot the fake statement and bet points on their choice.

Rules & Criteria:

  1. Each team started with 1,000 points.
  2. Teams bet points on their pick. Correct guesses earned points; wrong guesses lost them.
  3. The team with the highest point total after 11 rounds won.

Winner: Chirag and Anant (B25)

3. Match the Theorems

A deduction game where teams matched real-world technologies to the historical mathematicians behind the underlying theorems.

Rules & Criteria:

  1. After each attempt, judges shared only the number of correct matches, not which ones were right.
  2. Teams used the feedback to swap pairs and eliminate wrong options.
  3. The first team to find all correct matches won.

Winner: Eswaran and Siddhesh (B26)
