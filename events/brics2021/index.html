The Fourth BRICS Mathematics Conference

IISER THIRUVANANTHAPURAM

CMIT was extremely glad to be one of the local co-organizers of the fourth BRICS Mathematics Conference at IISER Thiruvananthapuram alongside the School of Mathematics.

About the Conference

The BRICS Mathematics Conference is an annual international event that aims to unite five countries whose mathematical schools are among world leaders—namely Brazil, Russia, India, China, and South Africa—to discuss and exchange ideas in various fields of Mathematics.

Visit the official conference portal here. All talks (both online and offline) are available as recorded videos on the BRICS 2021 Official YouTube Channel. You can also read a featured article about the event in the second edition of Donut.
