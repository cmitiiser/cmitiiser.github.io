CMIT S4 Outreach Program for Schools

SUNDAY, SEPTEMBER 18, 2022

The Club of Mathematics, IISER Thiruvananthapuram conducted an Outreach Program on September 18th, 2022. The aim of this program was to inculcate interest towards Mathematics in school kids and encourage mathematical creativity.

Registration and participation in the event were completely free of cost, allowing students to experience the IISER TVM campus and gain exposure to higher mathematics.
