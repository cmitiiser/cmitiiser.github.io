Meet the Team

CMIT is a student-run, faculty-supervised community officially recognized by the Mathematics Department at IISER Thiruvananthapuram. Have questions, event suggestions, or feedback? Want to propose problems for the Weekly Contest, volunteer as a peer math mentor, or give a Talk Series presentation? Reach out to us at mathsclub[at]iisertvm[dot]ac[dot]in
or contact any of our position bearers and faculty coordinators. Stay connected with us on our socials, below.
