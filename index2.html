Welcome to CMIT

The IISER Thiruvananthapuram Math Club

'Club of Mathematics, IISER Thiruvananthapuram' or CMIT, as we call it, is a student-run, department recognized, open Mathematics club — with objectives of promoting mathematics and providing an environment for the student community to discuss, learn and indulge in various activities throughout the year.
